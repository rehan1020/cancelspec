# Takedown Policy

## Contact

Send takedown requests to cancelspec@protonmail.com (as specified in `project.config`).

## Process

1. **Acknowledge** within 3 business days.
2. **Fact-check** the claim against our records and sources.
3. **Correct or remove** valid claims within 7 business days.
4. **Log the outcome** publicly with personal details redacted.

Platform-specific notices follow GitHub and Cloudflare processes.

## What we publish

CancelSpec publishes factual, sourced information about how to cancel subscriptions. We do not publish:

- Legal advice
- Company logos or brand assets
- Personal information
- Unverified claims
