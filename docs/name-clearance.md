## 3. Third-Party Brand & Trademark Policy (Nominative Fair Use)

CancelSpec catalogs and guides users through cancelling proprietary third-party subscription services (e.g., Netflix, Spotify, Hulu, The New York Times, Disney+ Hotstar, Deezer, etc.).

To ensure absolute adherence to nominative fair use doctrine under US and international trademark law:
1. **Identification Only**: Third-party trademarks and trade names appear strictly in plain text as necessary to identify the service.
2. **No Logos or Brand Assets**: No proprietary icons, wordmarks, logotypes, brand color schemes, or trade dress are stored, rendered, or bundled in this repository or static site output.
3. **No Endorsement or Affiliation**: Every page and documentation file carries the mandatory disclaimer:
   > *"Informational only, not legal advice. Service names belong to their owners and are used for identification only. CancelSpec is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any listed service."*
4. **Forks and Downstream Distribution**: Downstream redistributors and forks must adhere to [TRADEMARK.md](../policies/TRADEMARK.md) and may not use the CancelSpec logo or represent their forks as official releases.
