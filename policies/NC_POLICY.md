# NonCommercial Policy

The playbook corpus is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. This document clarifies what "NonCommercial" means for this project.

## Allowed

- Personal use
- Use by nonprofits and charities
- Use by schools and educational institutions
- Academic research
- Free, ad-free tools and subscription trackers
- Internal staff use within any organization

## Not allowed

- Paid products or services incorporating the data
- Ad-supported or affiliate-link websites using the data
- Selling or bundling the data
- Any use primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation

## Commercial licensing

The default answer to commercial license requests is no, because individual contributors hold copyright over their contributions.

## The specification

The specification itself (schema, field definitions, enums) is licensed under CC BY 4.0, which does permit commercial use.
