# Accessibility Statement

## Commitment

CancelSpec is committed to WCAG 2.2 AA conformance on every page and tool state.

## Standards

- Ordered headings and landmarks
- Visible focus indicators with 3:1 contrast
- Touch targets at least 24px (aiming for 44px)
- Text contrast 4.5:1 or higher
- No color-only signals
- Respects prefers-reduced-motion, prefers-color-scheme, and forced-colors
- Reflow at 400% zoom without horizontal scroll
- Live-region announcements for dynamic content
- Service pages work fully without JavaScript
- Print stylesheets for service pages and checklists

## Known issues

None currently documented.

## Reporting a barrier

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please open an issue on GitHub or email cancelspec@protonmail.com. We will respond within 7 days.
